# LuminaRail Brand Guidelines & Logo System

## Positioning & Core Concept
**LuminaRail** is stablecoin settlement infrastructure connecting local fiat payment rails with programmable USDC settlement on Stellar & Soroban.

### Symbol Concept: RAIL + FLOW + SETTLEMENT + STELLAR
The LuminaRail icon represents value moving along a reliable financial rail.

- **Twin Geometric Rail Tracks:** Visualizes local fiat payment rails flowing seamlessly into on-chain programmable value.
- **L + R Monogram Silhouette:** The outer vertical stem and base rail form a clean **"L"**, while the upper loop and diagonal forward rail construct an integrated **"R"**.
- **Stellar Settlement Node:** A 4-pointed glowing star located at the central rail convergence point symbolizes instant Stellar USDC settlement.

## Logo System Variants

All SVG assets are located in `/public/brand/` and are clean vector drawings scalable from 16px favicons to high-resolution vector displays.

### 1. Primary Full Logo (`luminarail-logo.svg`)
- **Usage:** Main navbar, hero sections, documentation, presentation headers.
- **Composition:** Icon symbol + "LuminaRail" wordmark.

### 2. Dark Background Logo (`luminarail-logo-dark.svg`)
- **Usage:** Dark mode web dashboard, dark background headers, social media cards.
- **Colors:** Green (`#15E113`) & Off-white (`#F8FAFC`) on dark navy (`#080D18`).

### 3. Light Background Logo (`luminarail-logo-light.svg`)
- **Usage:** Light mode web pages, white document headers, light background assets.
- **Colors:** Deep Navy (`#080D18`) & Green (`#15E113`).

### 4. Standalone Icon (`luminarail-icon.svg`, `luminarail-icon-dark.svg`, `luminarail-icon-light.svg`)
- **Usage:** 16px/32px/48px App icons, favicons, avatar badges, responsive mobile navbars.

### 5. Wordmark Only (`luminarail-wordmark.svg`)
- **Usage:** Compact footers, horizontal navigation where space is restricted.

### 6. Monochrome Version (`luminarail-logo-monochrome.svg`, `luminarail-icon-monochrome.svg`)
- **Usage:** Single-color print documents, black & white media, embossed stamps.

## Typography Guidelines
- **Primary Typeface:** Clean Modern Geometric Sans-Serif (`Inter`, `Geist Sans`, or system geometric sans).
- **Wordmark Structure:**
  - **"Lumina"**: ExtraBold (`800`), `#F8FAFC` (Dark Mode) or `#080D18` (Light Mode).
  - **"Rail"**: Medium (`500`/`600`), `#15E113` Green Accent.
- **Key Directive:** Do not substitute with low-readability futuristic or script fonts. Capital "L" and "R" must remain sharp and institutional.

---

## Correct & Incorrect Usage

### Correct Usage
- Maintain minimum clear space equal to 50% of icon height around the logo.
- Use `luminarail-logo-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds (`#080D18` / `#090D16` / `#111827`).
- Use `luminarail-icon.svg` as a 16x16px or 32x32px favicon.

### Incorrect Usage
- ❌ Do not skew, stretch, or alter the aspect ratio of the logo.
- ❌ Do not use non-brand colors (e.g. neon yellow, pink, generic blue).
- ❌ Do not add heavy drop shadows or glow effects outside the defined SVG system.
- ❌ Do not obscure the central Stellar settlement node.
